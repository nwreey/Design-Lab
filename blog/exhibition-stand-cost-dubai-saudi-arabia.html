How Much Does an Exhibition Stand Cost in Dubai & Saudi Arabia? (2026 Guide)

DesignsLab AI · August 2026 · 6 min read

If you are planning a stand at GITEX, Arab Health, Cityscape, LEAP, or Big 5, the first question is almost always the same: what will it cost? Exhibition stand pricing in Dubai and Saudi Arabia varies enormously — the same 100 sqm footprint can cost $30,000 or $300,000 — and most of that difference is decided at the design stage, before a single panel is built.

Typical price ranges in the GCC (2026)

Prices below are indicative market ranges for custom-built stands, based on what contractors in Dubai and Riyadh commonly quote. Shell scheme and modular systems cost significantly less; premium double-deck builds cost more.

  • Small inline stand (9–18 sqm): roughly $250–$450 per sqm — $3,000 to $8,000 all-in for a simple custom build.
  • Mid-size stand (36–60 sqm): roughly $350–$600 per sqm — $15,000 to $35,000 depending on finishes, AV, and hospitality areas.
  • Large island stand (100–200 sqm): $450–$800+ per sqm — $50,000 to $150,000 for a single-level build with meeting rooms and full branding.
  • Double-deck stands: add 40–70% over a single-level build of the same footprint, driven by structural engineering, stairs, approvals, and extra fit-out.

Saudi Arabia tends to run 10–20% above Dubai on labour and logistics for equivalent builds, though the gap is closing as local production capacity grows around Riyadh Front and Riyadh Exhibition & Convention Center.

What actually drives the price

Materials and size matter, but five design decisions move the budget more than anything else: the number of enclosed rooms (each meeting room adds walls, doors, HVAC, and lighting), rigging and hanging structures (venue rigging fees in the GCC are substantial), custom joinery versus system walls, AV walls and LED surfaces, and double-deck construction. A designer who knows these levers can often cut 20–30% from a quote without visibly changing the concept.

Where design fees fit in

Traditional stand design in the region costs $1,500–$8,000 per concept and takes one to three weeks — and if the client rejects the direction, much of that spend repeats. Practical ways to control cost

Book early — space and contractor rates both rise close to show dates. Reuse structural elements across shows while refreshing graphics. Choose one hero element (an LED wall, a striking canopy) instead of spreading budget thinly. Keep enclosed rooms to what you will actually use. And lock the design early: late changes on site are the most expensive square metres you will ever buy.
